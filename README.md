Shaf & Nodesha Retro Photo Booth — iPhone camera update

Upload index.html, script.js, styles.css to the root of your GitHub Pages repository, replacing existing files.

Tap OPEN CAMERA, allow permission, tap FRONT/REAR to switch cameras, then TAKE 4 PHOTOS. Images are captured at the highest resolution supported up to 2400px wide and exported as a 1600px wide PNG strip. Camera quality depends on iPhone and lighting. Use Safari over HTTPS.

No photos are uploaded to a server.
